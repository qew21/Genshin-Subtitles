# Dark-screen and dialogue-option scans are extra paths

Cutscene cards and Genshin dialogue choices are not region pairs. They ride the shared OCR clock as extra dispatch paths so pairs keep diffing and clearing; whole-beat preemption was rejected because it froze speaker-line pairs while choices were on screen. Placement defaults to the detected band (dark-screen) or an extra line on the voice-primary display (choice echo). Optional dedicated display regions can pin them without joining the pair list.
